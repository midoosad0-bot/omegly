Terms & Privacy

Last updated: November 2026

Who can use Omegly

Omegly is for people aged 18 or older. If you are under 18, you must not use this site. We may remove anyone we believe is under 18.

Rules

  • Be respectful. Harassment, hate, threats and bullying are not allowed.
  • No sexual content, nudity or sexual requests, in text or on camera.
  • Never share, request or ask about anything involving minors in a sexual or unsafe way. Such activity is reported to the authorities where the law requires it.
  • No spam, advertising, scams or links to other sites (links are removed automatically).
  • Do not record, screenshot or share other people's video or messages without their consent.
  • Do not share personal information such as your full name, address, phone number or passwords.

Reports and bans

If someone breaks the rules, press Report. The last messages of that conversation are sent to the site admin for review. The admin may ban the user's IP address. If you were banned by mistake, contact the admin from the site.

What we collect

  • Chat messages: they are passed between the two users and are not stored, except the last few messages of a conversation that is reported.
  • Video and audio: they go directly between the two users' devices (peer to peer) and are not recorded by Omegly. Because of this, your IP address is visible to the person you are connected to during a video chat.
  • IP address: used to apply bans and prevent abuse.
  • Messages to the admin: stored so the admin can read and reply to them.
  • On your device: we save your 18+ confirmation and a random support code in your browser's local storage. We do not use advertising trackers.

Safety tips

Strangers can be anyone. Do not click links, do not send money, and leave the chat or press Next whenever you feel uncomfortable.

No guarantees

Omegly is provided as is. We cannot control what other users say or do, and we are not responsible for their behavior, but we work to remove abusive users quickly.

Contact

Use the "Contact admin" button on the main page.
